Frequently Asked Questions

When will Signstimator be available?

Signstimator is built for iPhone. The App Store listing is still coming soon, and this site will link to it when the address is ready. Lightbox and awning estimating are coming soon inside the app. Check back here for updates.

What types of signs will the estimator cover?

Channel letters, ADA signs, vehicle wraps, adhesives, and materials. Each calculator uses the prices you enter. Lightbox and awning estimating are coming soon.

Will it work offline?

Yes — estimating calculations are designed to work on-device so you can price jobs even without a strong internet connection.

Can I customize pricing and markup?

Yes. The calculators run on your own price settings, and you can add a company logo from your photo library. The app does not ask for the camera or the microphone. Premium adds price profiles and sharing, hourly install and labor, materials by the sheet, and the other Premium tools listed on the home page.
